The Physiology

Pulse Transit Time (PTT) Explained

How combining simultaneous ECG and Photoplethysmography allows cuffless blood pressure estimation based on arterial wave dynamics.

STAGE 01

ECG R-Peak (Heart Spike)

When the heart's ventricles contract, a rapid electrical impulse generates the R-peak in the ECG signal, establishing t_0 ($t_{\text{ECG\_R}}$).

STAGE 02

Pulse Wave Propagation

The stroke volume ejects into the aorta, propelling a physical pressure wave through the arterial tree down to the radial artery in the wrist.

STAGE 03

Optical PPG Arrival

The watch's optical BioActive LED detects the sudden increase in blood volume (pulse wave foot), giving $t_{\text{PPG\_foot}}$.

STAGE 04

Regression Estimation

$\Delta t = t_{\text{PPG}} - t_{\text{ECG}}$. Higher pressure stiffens arterial walls, causing faster waves (shorter $\Delta t$). Calibrated regression calculates SYS and DIA.

Installation

How to Install on Phone & Galaxy Watch

Choose your preferred installation method below. No computer required if installing from your phone!

Installing `pulseforge-phone-app.apk` on Android:

  1. Download pulseforge-phone-app.apk from GitHub Releases directly on your smartphone.
  2. Open the downloaded file in your browser or file manager and tap Install.
  3. If prompted, enable "Install unknown apps" for your browser.
  4. Launch PulseForge and grant Bluetooth and notification permissions.

Installing on Galaxy Watch using your Phone (Recommended):

  1. Install GeminiMan Wear OS Manager or Easy Fire Tools from Google Play Store on your phone.
  2. Download pulseforge-galaxy-watch-app.apk from GitHub Releases onto your phone.
  3. On Galaxy Watch: Settings → About watch → Software info → Tap "Software version" 7 times to enable Developer options.
  4. Go to Settings → Developer options → turn ON ADB debugging & Wireless debugging (watch and phone on same Wi-Fi).
  5. In your phone manager app, enter the watch's IP and port, choose "Sideload APK", select the APK, and tap Install.

Installing on Galaxy Watch via Computer Terminal (ADB):

  1. Enable Wireless Debugging in Developer options on your Galaxy Watch.
  2. Note the watch IP address & port (e.g. 192.168.1.45:5555).
  3. Run the following commands in your PC terminal:
adb connect 192.168.1.45:5555
adb install pulseforge-galaxy-watch-app.apk
